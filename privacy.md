# Lectern Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Lectern is made by Nicholas Pidzarko. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Lectern on iPhone and iPad.

## The short version

Lectern does not collect your data. We do not run accounts, we do not have a server that stores your recordings or notes, and we do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Your recordings, transcripts, and notes

Everything you create in Lectern stays on your device. This includes lecture recordings, transcripts, notes, slides and files you import, flashcards, and study history.

If you have iCloud turned on, Lectern syncs this content between your devices using your own private iCloud account. That data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is not accessible to us. You can turn off iCloud sync for Lectern at any time in your device Settings.

## Microphone and speech recognition

Lectern asks for permission to use your microphone so it can record lectures. It uses Apple's speech recognition to create transcripts. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process audio to provide speech recognition, as described in Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your audio.

You can turn off microphone or speech recognition access at any time in Settings.

## Notes and flashcards

Lectern creates notes, flashcards, and quizzes from your transcripts on your device. This content is not sent to us.

## Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, usage data, or diagnostics. We do not sell or share any data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

## If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and do not share it.

## Children

Lectern is designed for students but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a recording, note, or notebook in Lectern removes it from your device and from your iCloud sync. Deleting the app removes all Lectern data stored on that device.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
